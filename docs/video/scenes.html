A real plan from testing

Design
Homepage hero: autoplay video + parallax
Scale
~2M visits a month
Verdict: Build differently
impact-plans/homepage-hero.md

The trimtabs

  1. Make a still poster the default hero, and treat the video as an enhancement. Load video only on wider screens, when Save-Data, reduced data and reduced motion are off. Everyone else keeps the poster, with an optional "Play film" button.
  2. Cap the video itself: short, silent, small, and it stops. About a 6–10 s loop, no audio, ≤ 720p on medium screens, pause when out of view, visible pause control.
  3. Encode motion limits in the design tokens. A reduced motion mode with parallax depth 0, plus media budgets per breakpoint.
Decide before the shoot, while it's still cheap to change.

NFC chip in every jar

500,000 tags that open the same experience. Most never tapped, all headed for recycling streams that can't handle them.

→
Trimtab

Printed QR + short URL

Same result in hand, no extra material. Keep NFC only where it's reused: testers, displays, refill jars.

Brand-owned redirect URL

Printed packaging can't be updated. If the app or vendor changes, half a million jars shouldn't become dead links.
